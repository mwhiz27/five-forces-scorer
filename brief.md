6. Biggest Trend (Next 5 Years)
Structural migration of live sports rights from broadcast/cable to streaming. Platforms spent
$14.2 billion on sports rights in 2026 (up from $13.2 billion in 2025), with landmark exclusive
multi-year deals across NBA, NFL, Formula 1, UFC, and WWE locking streaming as the primary
distributor of premium live sports for the next decade.
Source: IBISWorld Industry Report 516210, Industry Outlook section — "Live sports have
become the defining competitive battleground for US streaming platforms... Streaming services
will collectively spend $14.2 billion on sports rights in 2026, up from $13.2 billion in 2025,
according to Ampere Analysis data."
7. Biggest Threat
Sustained structural decline in cable TV subscriptions, which compresses TV airtime revenue
(36.8% of industry revenue). Cable subscriptions declining ~2.4% annually; TV airtime prices fell
nearly 8% in 2025 alone (5-year CAGR of -10.6%). Digital streaming growth has only partially
offset this permanent, compounding revenue loss.
Source: IBISWorld Industry Report 516210, Key External Drivers section — "TV airtime prices
plunged nearly 8% in 2025. Content providers are aggressively investing in alternative ad
opportunities and digital streaming formats... The dramatic downturn in TV advertising's
five-year CAGR of -10.6% is forcing a fundamental rethink in channel strategy."
